United Nations University – Centre for Policy Research

Global Digital Governance Lab

I'm a founder of the Global Digital Governance Lab, a collaboration with the Graduate Institute that bridges scholarship and international policymaking to serve as a platform for global conversations on digital governance.

Digital Sovereignty Observatory

I'm a co-founder and coordinator of the Digital Sovereignty Observatory (DSO), which conducts interdisciplinary research, policy, and advocacy across four dimensions of digital sovereignty: individual, organisational, state, international. It's a collaboration with the University of Virginia, King's College London, Queen Mary University of London, the Graduate Institute of Geneva, Caribou, and the United Nations University – Centre for Policy Research.
